# Planets in the signs (Graha in Rashi) — a plain-language primer

A concept primer written for Vedic Astrology in our own words, to deepen how a reading describes a
planet placed in a sign. Reference material for the AI writer (retrieval), never used in
calculation. Each line is a tendency, not a fixed fate, and is always read together with the
planet's house, strength, aspects and dignity. Where a sign is a planet's own, exaltation or
debilitation, it is noted, since that strengthens or softens the effect.

## The Sun (Surya) — identity, confidence, vitality, the father

Aries (exalted): strong will, leadership and pioneering confidence. Taurus: steady, determined, fond
of comfort and stability. Gemini: curious, communicative, versatile in expression. Cancer:
sensitive, attached to home and mother, changeable moods. Leo (own sign): dignified, generous, a
natural authority. Virgo: analytical, modest, service-minded, self-critical. Libra (debilitated):
seeks harmony and partnership, but can lean on others' approval to feel sure of itself. Scorpio:
intense, determined, private, investigative. Sagittarius: principled, honest, optimistic,
philosophical. Capricorn: ambitious, disciplined, status-aware, hard-working. Aquarius: independent,
humanitarian, unconventional. Pisces: compassionate, imaginative, idealistic.

## The Moon (Chandra) — the mind, emotions, comfort, the mother

Aries: quick, independent, impulsive feelings. Taurus (exalted): calm, steady, contented, loyal.
Gemini: restless, curious, communicative mind. Cancer (own sign): nurturing, sensitive, attached to
home and family. Leo: warm, proud, wants recognition and affection. Virgo: practical, analytical, a
tendency to worry. Libra: sociable, refined, seeks balance in relationships. Scorpio (debilitated):
intense emotions and resilience, but strong ups and downs that ask for steadiness. Sagittarius:
optimistic, free-spirited, restless for meaning. Capricorn: reserved, serious, self-contained.
Aquarius: independent, humane, a little detached. Pisces: compassionate, dreamy, deeply
impressionable.

## Mars (Mangala) — energy, courage, drive, action

Aries (own sign): bold, assertive, pioneering, competitive. Taurus: patient but stubborn effort,
sensual drive. Gemini: mentally energetic, quick to debate. Cancer (debilitated): drive runs through
feeling and defence, better channelled through patience than force. Leo: forceful, proud, natural
leadership energy. Virgo: precise, hard-working, technically skilled. Libra: acts through
partnership and diplomacy. Scorpio (own sign): intense, strategic, penetrating, determined.
Sagittarius: adventurous, enthusiastic, principled action. Capricorn (exalted): disciplined,
controlled, highly effective ambition. Aquarius: reformist, unconventional energy. Pisces:
compassionate or restless action, better with a clear aim.

## Mercury (Budha) — intellect, speech, skill, commerce

Aries: quick, sharp, sometimes hasty thought and speech. Taurus: steady, practical, deliberate
thinking. Gemini (own sign): versatile, clever, witty, communicative. Cancer: intuitive, emotional,
strong memory. Leo: confident, dignified expression. Virgo (own sign and exalted): analytical,
precise, discriminating, highly skilled. Libra: balanced, diplomatic, artistic reasoning. Scorpio:
penetrating, research-minded, private. Sagittarius (debilitated): sees the big picture but can skip
detail; blunt and philosophical. Capricorn: organised, methodical, practical. Aquarius: inventive,
original, abstract. Pisces: imaginative and intuitive, best with a little structure.

## Jupiter (Guru) — wisdom, fortune, faith, teachers, children

Aries: enthusiastic, principled, pioneering faith. Taurus: generous, values wealth and comfort,
practical wisdom. Gemini: versatile knowledge, a gift for teaching and communication. Cancer
(exalted): compassionate, wise, nurturing, devout — Jupiter at its most benevolent. Leo: dignified,
generous, a guiding authority. Virgo: detailed and service-minded, less expansive but careful.
Libra: just, balanced, partnership-minded wisdom. Scorpio: deep, researching, drawn to the hidden.
Sagittarius (own sign): philosophical, dharmic, optimistic, a natural teacher. Capricorn
(debilitated): cautious and pragmatic, duty before idealism; faith grows slowly but solidly.
Aquarius: humanitarian, reform-minded wisdom. Pisces (own sign): spiritual, devotional,
compassionate, imaginative.

## Venus (Shukra) — love, beauty, comfort, art, relationships

Aries: ardent, impulsive, passionate in love. Taurus (own sign): sensual, loyal, artistic,
comfort-loving. Gemini: playful, intellectual, versatile in taste and affection. Cancer: tender,
nurturing, home-loving. Leo: romantic, dramatic, warm and loyal. Virgo (debilitated): modest and
discriminating; affection is shown through care and service rather than display. Libra (own sign):
refined, harmonious, relationship-focused, artistic. Scorpio: passionate, intense, can be possessive.
Sagittarius: idealistic, free, philosophical in love. Capricorn: loyal, practical, status-aware
affection. Aquarius: friendly, unconventional, a little detached. Pisces (exalted): compassionate,
romantic, devotional, selfless love.

## Saturn (Shani) — discipline, endurance, duty, longevity

Aries (debilitated): chafes against limits; self-doubt matures into hard-won assertiveness. Taurus:
patient, enduring, practical accumulation. Gemini: disciplined, methodical mind and communication.
Cancer: emotional restraint and duty to home, security earned over time. Leo: a tension between pride
and duty that ripens into real authority. Virgo: meticulous, service-minded discipline. Libra
(exalted): fair, responsible and just, especially in partnership — Saturn at its most balanced.
Scorpio: deep endurance and control, strength through intensity. Sagittarius: disciplined ethics,
cautious philosophy. Capricorn (own sign): ambitious, structured, dutiful, status through sustained
effort. Aquarius (own sign): humane, reform-minded, responsible but detached. Pisces: disciplined
compassion, duty to the vulnerable, spiritual endurance.

## Rahu and Ketu in signs

Rahu (the north node) amplifies and makes worldly the sign it sits in — giving ambition, fascination
and a hunger to master that sign's affairs, sometimes in excess. Ketu (the south node) withdraws and
spiritualises its sign — giving instinctive skill there from the past but little worldly appetite for
it, turning attention inward. Both are always read through the houses they occupy and the planets
they join.

## Reading a planet in a sign

The sign is the planet's costume — the style in which it acts. A strong dignity (own sign or
exaltation) lets the planet give its best; debilitation asks for patience and often improves when the
dispositor is strong (neecha bhanga). None of this is destiny: it describes a leaning to work with,
alongside the house the planet sits in and the company it keeps.
