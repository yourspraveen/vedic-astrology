# The 27 Nakshatras — a plain-language primer

A concept primer written for Vedic Astrology, in our own words, to help explain terms a reading may
use. It is reference material for the optional AI writer (retrieval), not a source of calculations;
positions and dasha periods always come from the program's own astronomy. Nakshatra lords, deities
and symbols follow standard Parashari convention.

## What a nakshatra is

The zodiac of 360° is divided two ways in Vedic astrology. The twelve **rashis** (signs) of 30°
each are the familiar one. Alongside them run the twenty-seven **nakshatras** (lunar mansions) of
13°20′ each — the band of stars the Moon passes through on its monthly journey. Each nakshatra has a
ruling planet, a presiding deity, a symbol and a characteristic theme, and each is divided into four
quarters called **padas** of 3°20′.

The nakshatra the Moon occupies at birth is your **janma nakshatra** (birth star). It sets the
starting planet of the Vimshottari dasha cycle and colours the mind and instincts. A planet's
nakshatra refines what its sign already says: the sign is the broad temperament, the nakshatra the
finer grain.

## The 27 nakshatras

Ashwini (0°–13°20′ Aries) — ruled by Ketu, deity the Ashwini Kumaras (celestial healers), symbol a
horse's head. The theme is swift beginnings, speed, healing and pioneering energy; a quick starter
who heals and moves on.

Bharani (13°20′–26°40′ Aries) — ruled by Venus, deity Yama (lord of restraint and the cycle of
life), symbol the yoni. The theme is endurance, bearing burdens, discipline and transformation
through holding firm.

Krittika (26°40′ Aries–10° Taurus) — ruled by the Sun, deity Agni (fire), symbol a blade or flame.
The theme is sharpness, purification, cutting through illusion and focused ambition.

Rohini (10°–23°20′ Taurus) — ruled by the Moon, deity Brahma/Prajapati the creator, symbol an ox
cart or chariot. The theme is growth, beauty, fertility, material comfort and the pull to create and
nurture.

Mrigashira (23°20′ Taurus–6°40′ Gemini) — ruled by Mars, deity Soma (the Moon), symbol a deer's
head. The theme is searching, curiosity, gentleness and a restless quest for something better.

Ardra (6°40′–20° Gemini) — ruled by Rahu, deity Rudra (the storm), symbol a teardrop. The theme is
upheaval that clears the ground for renewal — emotional storms, then fresh clarity.

Punarvasu (20° Gemini–3°20′ Cancer) — ruled by Jupiter, deity Aditi (the boundless mother), symbol a
bow and quiver. The theme is return, renewal, safe homecoming and steady optimism after difficulty.

Pushya (3°20′–16°40′ Cancer) — ruled by Saturn, deity Brihaspati (teacher of the gods), symbol a
cow's udder or lotus. Often called the most auspicious nakshatra; the theme is nourishment, care,
and spiritual and material support.

Ashlesha (16°40′–30° Cancer) — ruled by Mercury, deity the Nagas (serpents), symbol a coiled
serpent. The theme is insight, binding, intuition and the hidden, hypnotic power of the mind.

Magha (0°–13°20′ Leo) — ruled by Ketu, deity the Pitris (ancestors), symbol a throne. The theme is
lineage, inherited authority, tradition, honour and leadership.

Purva Phalguni (13°20′–26°40′ Leo) — ruled by Venus, deity Bhaga (delight and fortune), symbol the
front of a bed or a hammock. The theme is pleasure, rest, creativity, romance and enjoyment.

Uttara Phalguni (26°40′ Leo–10° Virgo) — ruled by the Sun, deity Aryaman (patron of friendship and
contracts), symbol the back of a bed. The theme is steady friendship, patronage, help freely given
and reliable partnership.

Hasta (10°–23°20′ Virgo) — ruled by the Moon, deity Savitar (the Sun's creative light), symbol an
open hand. The theme is skill with the hands, craft, cleverness and the ability to grasp and shape.

Chitra (23°20′ Virgo–6°40′ Libra) — ruled by Mars, deity Tvashtar/Vishwakarma (divine architect),
symbol a bright jewel or pearl. The theme is craftsmanship, beauty, brilliance and making things
well.

Swati (6°40′–20° Libra) — ruled by Rahu, deity Vayu (the wind), symbol a young shoot swaying in the
breeze. The theme is independence, movement, flexibility, trade and self-reliance.

Vishakha (20° Libra–3°20′ Scorpio) — ruled by Jupiter, deity Indra-Agni, symbol a triumphal arch or
potter's wheel. The theme is goal-focus, determination and the patient effort that reaches a chosen
aim.

Anuradha (3°20′–16°40′ Scorpio) — ruled by Saturn, deity Mitra (friendship and harmony), symbol a
lotus or staff. The theme is devotion, cooperation, loyal friendship and finding balance under
pressure.

Jyeshtha (16°40′–30° Scorpio) — ruled by Mercury, deity Indra (king of the gods), symbol an earring
or umbrella. The theme is seniority, responsibility, protectiveness and quiet, hidden power.

Mula (0°–13°20′ Sagittarius) — ruled by Ketu, deity Nirriti (dissolution), symbol a bunch of tied
roots. The theme is getting to the root of things — letting the old dissolve so truth can be found.

Purva Ashadha (13°20′–26°40′ Sagittarius) — ruled by Venus, deity Apas (the waters), symbol a
winnowing basket or fan. The theme is invigoration, conviction, early victory and the courage of
belief.

Uttara Ashadha (26°40′ Sagittarius–10° Capricorn) — ruled by the Sun, deity the Vishwadevas (the
universal gods), symbol an elephant's tusk. The theme is lasting victory, integrity and achievement
that endures.

Shravana (10°–23°20′ Capricorn) — ruled by the Moon, deity Vishnu, symbol an ear or three
footprints. The theme is listening, learning, connection and wisdom gained through hearing.

Dhanishta (23°20′ Capricorn–6°40′ Aquarius) — ruled by Mars, deity the eight Vasus, symbol a drum or
flute. The theme is rhythm, wealth, music, fame and adaptability.

Shatabhisha (6°40′–20° Aquarius) — ruled by Rahu, deity Varuna (cosmic waters and order), symbol an
empty circle or "a hundred healers". The theme is healing, mystery, solitude and restoring order.

Purva Bhadrapada (20° Aquarius–3°20′ Pisces) — ruled by Jupiter, deity Aja Ekapada, symbol the front
of a funeral cot or a two-faced figure. The theme is intensity, idealism and transformation through
facing extremes.

Uttara Bhadrapada (3°20′–16°40′ Pisces) — ruled by Saturn, deity Ahir Budhnya (serpent of the
deep), symbol the back of a funeral cot. The theme is depth, patience, calm wisdom and inner
steadiness.

Revati (16°40′–30° Pisces) — ruled by Mercury, deity Pushan (the nourisher and guide of journeys),
symbol a fish or drum. The theme is completion, nourishment, safe travel and gentle compassion — the
journey's end that readies a new beginning.

## How a reading uses this

When a report names your birth star, or a planet's nakshatra, it is pointing to the finer texture
above. The ruling planet links the nakshatra into the Vimshottari dasha timeline; the deity and
symbol give its flavour. None of this is fate — it describes tendencies and the grain of a
temperament, to be read alongside the sign, house and planetary strengths the rest of the report
works out.
