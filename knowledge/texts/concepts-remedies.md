# Planetary remedies, mantras and charity (Daan) — a plain-language primer

A concept primer written for Vedic Astrology in our own words, to help explain the remedial practices a
reading may mention. Reference material for the AI writer (retrieval), never used in calculation.
These are traditional, devotional practices a person may choose to follow for peace of mind and
focus — not calculated facts, medical advice, obligations or guaranteed outcomes.

## How remedies are understood

Remedies (upaya) are gentle ways to turn attention toward a planet that is troubling a chart — by
devotion, discipline and generosity rather than by force. The common forms are a mantra (repeated
sound), worship of the planet's deity, fasting on the planet's weekday, charity (daan) of items
linked to the planet, and wearing its colour. They are offered as a steadying daily practice; their
real value is often the focus, patience and goodwill they cultivate.

## The nine planets

Sun (Surya) — weekday Sunday. Seed (beej) mantra: *Om Hraam Hreem Hroum Sah Suryaya Namah*. Deity
Shiva or Surya. Colour red or orange. Charity: wheat, jaggery, copper, red cloth, given around
sunrise. Serving one's father and elders is a traditional remedy.

Moon (Chandra) — weekday Monday. Seed mantra: *Om Shraam Shreem Shroum Sah Chandraya Namah*. Deity
Parvati or Gauri. Colour white. Charity: rice, milk, silver, white cloth. Caring for one's mother
and keeping a calm routine are traditional remedies.

Mars (Mangala) — weekday Tuesday. Seed mantra: *Om Kraam Kreem Kroum Sah Bhaumaya Namah*. Deity
Hanuman or Kartikeya. Colour red. Charity: red lentils (masoor), red cloth, copper. Reciting the
Hanuman Chalisa is a common practice.

Mercury (Budha) — weekday Wednesday. Seed mantra: *Om Braam Breem Broum Sah Budhaya Namah*. Deity
Vishnu or Ganesha. Colour green. Charity: whole green gram (moong), green cloth, bronze. Giving to
students and helping with education suit Mercury.

Jupiter (Guru) — weekday Thursday. Seed mantra: *Om Graam Greem Groum Sah Gurave Namah*. Deity
Brihaspati or Vishnu. Colour yellow. Charity: turmeric, chana dal, gold, yellow cloth, books.
Respecting teachers and elders is a traditional Jupiter remedy.

Venus (Shukra) — weekday Friday. Seed mantra: *Om Draam Dreem Droum Sah Shukraya Namah*. Deity
Lakshmi. Colour white or pastel. Charity: sugar, rice, curd, silver, perfume, white clothing. Kindness
in relationships and appreciation of beauty and art suit Venus.

Saturn (Shani) — weekday Saturday. Seed mantra: *Om Praam Preem Proum Sah Shanaye Namah*. Deity Shani
or Hanuman. Colour dark blue or black. Charity: black sesame (til), mustard oil, iron, black cloth,
and service to workers and the elderly. Lighting a lamp on Saturday and honest, patient work are the
heart of Saturn's remedy.

Rahu — weekday Saturday. Seed mantra: *Om Bhraam Bhreem Bhroum Sah Rahave Namah*. Deity Durga. Colour
smoky or multicoloured. Charity: black gram (urad), a blanket, coconut, mustard oil. Honouring one's
word and avoiding shortcuts suit Rahu.

Ketu — weekday Tuesday. Seed mantra: *Om Sraam Sreem Sroum Sah Ketave Namah*. Deity Ganesha. Colour
grey or multicoloured. Charity: a blanket, sesame, feeding dogs. Quiet spiritual practice and
detachment suit Ketu.

## Fasting (Vratham)

Fasting on a planet's weekday is a traditional act of restraint meant to steady the body and mind. It
is kept partially or fully by choice, with simple natural food, and is understood as a devotional
discipline rather than a prescription. A reading should describe it as an optional practice a person
may take up if it brings them peace, never as a rule or a cure.
