# Jyotisha concepts (Sanskrit terms explained)

Written for Vedic Astrology in plain words. It explains the Sanskrit terms the report uses, so the
AI writer (and anyone reading the knowledge folder) has the background. It quotes no verses or
translations.

## Graha (planet)

A graha is a "seizer": a body that takes hold of a part of life. Jyotisha uses nine: Surya (Sun),
Chandra (Moon), Mangala or Kuja (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Guru (Jupiter), Shukra (Venus), Shani
(Saturn) and the two lunar nodes, Rahu and Ketu, which are shadow planets (chhaya graha). Jupiter,
Venus, a waxing Moon and an unafflicted Mercury are natural benefics (saumya); Saturn, Mars, the Sun,
Rahu and Ketu are natural malefics (krura).

## Rashi (sign) and Lagna (ascendant)

The zodiac is divided into twelve rashis of 30 degrees each, counted in the sidereal (nirayana)
zodiac by subtracting the ayanamsa. The rashi rising in the east at birth is the Lagna; it becomes
the first house, Tanu Bhava, and sets the order of all the other houses.

## Bhava (house) and Bhavesha (house lord)

A bhava is a field of life. The lord of a bhava (bhavesha) is the ruler of the sign on it, and where
that lord sits links the two fields: the lord of the 2nd (Dhana) in the 5th (Putra), for example,
ties wealth to children and intelligence. Bhavas are grouped as kendra (1, 4, 7, 10), trikona (1, 5,
9), dusthana (6, 8, 12) and upachaya (3, 6, 10, 11). Each bhava also has a karaka, a natural
significator: Jupiter for children, Venus for marriage, Saturn for longevity.

## Dignity (bala of position)

A planet is strongest in its exaltation sign (uchcha), strong in its own sign (svakshetra) or its
moolatrikona, comfortable in a friend's sign (mitra kshetra), uneasy in an enemy's sign (shatru
kshetra) and weakest in its debilitation sign (neecha). Natural friendship (naisargika maitri)
between planets is fixed; temporary friendship (tatkalika maitri) depends on their distance in the
chart, and the two together give the five-fold (panchadha) relationship.

## Drishti (aspect)

Every planet aspects the 7th sign from itself. Mars also aspects the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th
and 9th, and Saturn the 3rd and 10th. Jupiter's aspect protects; Saturn's delays; Mars's energises
or inflames.

## Yoga (combination)

A yoga is a combination of planets that produces a definite result. Pancha Mahapurusha yogas (Ruchaka,
Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Sasa) arise when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn is in its own or
exaltation sign in a kendra. Gaja Kesari is Jupiter in a kendra from the Moon. Raja yogas join the
lords of kendras and trikonas; Dhana yogas join the lords of wealth houses. A yoga gives its results
mainly in the dashas of the planets that form it.

## Dosha (affliction)

A dosha is a flaw that needs care. Kuja (Mangal) dosha is Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or
12th house, judged mainly for marriage; Rahu and Ketu doshas are judged the same way. Jupiter's
aspect or presence softens them, and matching two charts with the same dosha balances them.

## Dasha (planetary period)

Vimshottari dasha divides a 120-year life into periods ruled by the nine planets in a fixed order:
Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19 and Mercury 17 years. The
first period is set by the Moon's nakshatra at birth, and the part of it already passed sets the
balance at birth. Each mahadasha divides into nine bhuktis (antardashas) in the same order, starting
with its own planet. A dasha gives the results of its planet's strength, the houses it rules, the
house it sits in and the yogas it joins.

## Nakshatra (lunar mansion)

The zodiac is also divided into 27 nakshatras of 13°20′, each with four padas. Each has a presiding
deity and a dasha lord, and the Moon's nakshatra at birth (janma nakshatra) colours the mind and
starts the dasha sequence. Counting from it gives the nine taras; the 3rd (Vipat), 5th (Pratyak) and
7th (Naidhana) are unfavourable.

## Panchanga (five limbs of time)

Vara (weekday), tithi (lunar day), nakshatra, yoga (sum of the Sun's and Moon's longitudes, 27 kinds)
and karana (half a tithi). Together they describe the quality of the moment of birth.

## Upaya (remedy)

Remedies strengthen a weak benefic or calm an afflicted planet: mantra japa, worship of the planet's
deity, fasting on its weekday, charity (dana) of its grains and colours, and gemstones only after
careful judgement.
